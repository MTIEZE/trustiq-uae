Closing your TrustIQ account

Last updated 28 August 2026.

From inside the app

  1. Open TrustIQ and sign in.
  2. Tap the menu at the top of your contract list.
  3. Choose Your account.
  4. Choose Close your account and confirm.

It happens immediately. You will not be able to sign in again, and the app tells you on the spot exactly what was kept.

What is erased

  • Your name.
  • Your email address, replaced by one that can never receive anything.
  • Your phone number, if you gave one.
  • Your identity verification, so nothing continues to vouch for you.
  • Your sign-in. The account cannot be reopened.

What is kept, and why

If nothing at all points at your account, it is deleted outright, row and all. That is the common case for somebody who signed up and went no further.

If you have been party to a contract, the contract stays. So do the documents filed against it, what each side said about any dispute, and the record of who did what and when. They are attached to a record that no longer names you.

This is not us keeping your data for ourselves. A contract belongs to two people. If closing an account erased it, anyone could destroy the other side's only record of an agreement by deleting their own account, and a record that can be made to disappear is worth nothing to the person relying on it.

How long the rest is kept

Contracts and the documents filed against them are kept for as long as they might be needed to settle a dispute about them, and are not deleted on a schedule. The record of your closure itself, which holds the address you asked from and nothing else about you, is kept so that a later question can be answered.

If you cannot get into the app

Write to tiezemohamed@gmail.com from the address on the account. We will do the same thing by hand and write back to say what was erased and what stayed, within thirty days.
